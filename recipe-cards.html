Spanish Chicken & Sweetcorn Stew

with smoky paprika, sweetcorn & mashed cannellini beans

Contains: Celery
1

Utensils

Slow cooker
Mixing bowl

Cooking steps

4 steps
1

Mash the beans

Rinse the beans and mash with a potato masher in a bowl until none are left whole, then tip into the slow cooker with the rest of the ingredients.

2

Rinse out the passata

Half-fill the empty passata carton with cold water, swill out the corners, and add that to the pot too — it keeps every bit of sauce out of the bin.

3

Cook low and slow

Stir well, flattening the chicken so it's under the liquid. Lid on, HIGH for 3 hours or LOW for 5–6 hours, until the chicken is very tender.

TIP: Whole sweetcorn kernels aren't a choking risk from 6 months, so this is fine as-is for littles — blend a portion smooth for younger babies.

4

Serve

Spoon into bowls with potatoes, pasta, rice, or soft flatbread for dunking, and a scatter of chopped herbs if you like.

Cooking steps

5 steps
1

Soften the veg

Finely dice the celery, onion and carrots. Cook in a large frying pan with a little oil for about 5 minutes until starting to soften, then tip into the slow cooker.

2

Flour and brown the beef

Coat the beef in the flour, shaking off the excess, and peel & quarter the potatoes. Brown the beef in batches in the same hot pan until dark and crispy outside, then add to the slow cooker too.

3

Fill and cook

Add the potatoes and the rest of the ingredients (bay leaves, garlic, mixed herbs, Worcestershire sauce) with the stock cubes dissolved in 800ml boiling water. Stir well, lid on, and cook 6–8 hours on LOW or 4–5 hours on HIGH, until the meat falls apart with gentle pressure.

4

Shape the dumplings

An hour before serving, mix the self-raising flour, suet and Cheddar with a pinch of pepper and about 160ml cold water into a slightly sticky dough. Roll into golf-ball-sized dumplings — about 10.

5

Finish uncovered

Stir the stew, drop the dumplings on top without overlapping, then cook uncovered on HIGH until they've doubled in size.

IMPORTANT: Keep the lid off once the dumplings go in — lifting it partway through stops them rising properly.

Cooking steps

4 steps
1

Brown the lamb in batches

Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large frying pan over high heat. Fry half the lamb in a single layer for 2 minutes until browned, turn for another 1–2 minutes, then move to the slow cooker. Repeat with the rest.

2

Add the aromatics

Grate or finely dice the onion, peel and chunk the carrots, and add both to the slow cooker with the cumin, paprika, chickpeas, tomatoes, stock cubes, garlic, cinnamon and raisins.

TIP: Deglaze the empty hot frying pan with 400ml boiling water, scraping up the browned bits, then pour that into the pot too — that's where a lot of the flavour is hiding.

3

Cook low and slow

Stir well, lid on, and cook for 4 hours on HIGH or 6–7 hours on LOW until the meat is super tender.

4

Serve

Spoon into bowls with boiled potatoes to soak up the juices, and fresh herbs to garnish if you like.

Cooking steps

5 steps
1

Soften the base

Peel and grate (or finely dice) the onion, and finely grate the garlic. Add both to the slow cooker with the oil, stir, lid on, and cook 10–15 minutes on HIGH.

2

Add the spice paste

Stir in the tomato purée, garam masala and curry powder. Lid back on for a further 10 minutes, until the kitchen smells aromatic.

3

Add the coconut milk and potatoes

Dice the potatoes into 3cm chunks. Add them with the coconut milk and the stock cube (dissolved in the empty coconut milk can, topped up with boiling water). Stir, lid on, and cook 3 hours on HIGH or 5 hours on LOW.

4

Stir in the spinach

Add the frozen spinach, lid back on, and cook a further 30 minutes on HIGH or 1 hour on LOW.

TIP: For littles, mash any potato chunks that are too big to hold — it's kept mild throughout, so season adult portions with salt at the table.

5

Serve

Pile into bowls with a flatbread tucked into the side of each.

Cooking steps

4 steps
1

Load the pot

Add the chicken thighs, grated courgette, garlic, a good grinding of pepper, and the butter to the slow cooker.

2

Cook until tender

Dissolve the stock cube in 550ml boiling water and add to the pot. Stir well so the chicken is submerged, lid on, and cook HIGH for 3 hours or LOW for 6 hours.

3

Stir through the sauce

Mix the cream cheese and milk in a bowl until smooth, then pour into the pot with the uncooked pasta. Stir well, squashing each piece of chicken with the spoon so it breaks up a little.

4

Finish and rest

Lid back on, cook 40 minutes on HIGH or 1 hour on LOW. Stir well, then let it stand with the lid on for 5 minutes before stirring again and serving.

TIP: For an even creamier, cheesier result, stir in the smoked Cheddar once everything's cooked and let it melt in during the 5-minute rest.

Cooking steps

4 steps
1

Cook the squash

Add the chopped butternut squash, onion and garlic to the slow cooker with a grinding of pepper. Crumble in the stock cube, add 500ml boiling water, stir, lid on, and cook 3 hours on HIGH or 5–6 hours on LOW until very tender.

2

Blend to a purée

Blend the veg and cooking liquid until very smooth, with a stick blender or in a food processor, then pour back into the slow cooker.

3

Cook the pasta in the sauce

Stir in the uncooked pasta, milk, cream cheese, smoked Cheddar and smoked paprika. Lid back on and cook 30–40 minutes on LOW, until the pasta is cooked.

TIP: Leftovers make a great pasta bake — pour into an oven dish with a splash more milk, top with extra cheese, and bake at 200°C fan for about 20 minutes until golden.

4

Serve

Spoon straight into bowls — adults can add a touch of salt and pepper to their own portion.
